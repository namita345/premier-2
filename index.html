02About Us

About Premier Container Group

Premier Container Group is a full-service shipping container construction company specializing in custom container homes, offices, and commercial buildings. We combine architectural design, structural engineering and skilled fabrication to deliver durable, sustainable and beautifully finished spaces.

Our team handles every stage of your project — design, permitting support, fabrication, finishes, delivery and installation. Whether you are planning a compact backyard studio or a multi-container residence, we build with the same commitment to quality, precision and craftsmanship.

03Custom Design

Custom Design & Planning

Every Premier Container Group project begins with design. Our in-house team develops detailed CAD drawings, floor plans and 3D renderings so you can see your container home before a single cut is made.

We work closely with you to plan layouts, window and door placement, insulation, electrical, plumbing and finishes — engineering each structure to meet local building codes and your unique vision.

04Our Process

How We Build

A clear, step-by-step process from first conversation to final installation. We keep you informed at every stage so your project stays on schedule and on budget.

01

Consultation

We discuss your goals, site, budget and timeline to determine the right container solution for your project.

02

Design & Engineering

Our team creates detailed CAD drawings, floor plans, renderings and structural engineering for approval and permitting.

03

Fabrication

Containers are cut, reinforced, welded, insulated and framed in our facility with precision and quality control.
